## About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the **University of Macau**, advised by [Prof. Chi-Man Pun](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=JTkP_EAAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) and [Prof. Qieshi Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=r59MMbUAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN).

My research covers visual place recognition (VPR) and representation learning. I am currently interested in representation learning tailored for visual perception tasks, especially for autonomous driving and UAV navigation.

## Education

- **PhD in Computer Science**, University of Macau, 2024 - Present.
- **MSc in Computer Technology**, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) & Shenzhen Institute of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, 2021 - 2024.
- **BTech in Computer Science and Technology**, Dongguan University of Technology, 2016 - 2020.
